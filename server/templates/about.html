Helen's Barber Shop opened its doors on Firestone Boulevard in 2008 with a simple idea: a great haircut should feel like a reset.

What started as one chair and a pair of clippers has grown into a neighborhood institution. Our team brings classic barbering technique, honest conversation, and an exacting eye to every appointment.
